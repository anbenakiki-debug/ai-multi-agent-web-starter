## Planning rules

- Milestones describe outcomes, not departments or vague effort.
- Mark a milestone at risk when a critical-path Issue is blocked or cannot meet its planned window.
- Do not silently move dates: document the cause, option, and human decision in the relevant Issue/ADR.
